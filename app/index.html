Walk with me

Your circle follows your live location until you arrive. If you don't tap "I've arrived" in time, they're alerted.

Keep Shecurity open while you walk. The screen stays on so your location keeps updating.

Check-in

Set how long you'll be. If you don't check in, your phone asks if you're OK, then alerts your circle with where you said you'd be.

Keep Shecurity open. A locked or dead phone can't raise the alarm, so tell someone your plan too.

    Help lines

    Free safety features already on your phone
    iPhone: Emergency SOS
    1. Open Settings, then Emergency SOS.
    2. Turn on "Call with Hold and Release" or "Call with 5 Button Presses".
    3. Add emergency contacts in the Health app under Medical ID. They get a text with your location after an SOS call.
    iPhone: Check In in Messages
    1. In a chat, tap +, then More, then Check In.
    2. If you don't arrive in time, your friend gets your location and battery automatically.
    3. Needs iOS 17 or newer on both phones.
    Android: Emergency SOS
    1. Open Settings, then Safety & emergency, then Emergency SOS.
    2. Pressing the power button 5 times calls emergency services and can share your location.
    3. On some phones: Settings, Advanced features, Send SOS messages.
    One-tap SOS from your home screen

    Your SOS link. Opening it starts a 5-second countdown you can cancel, then alerts your circle.

    iPhone: SOS widget or button on the home screen
    1. Copy your SOS link above.
    2. Open the Shortcuts app, tap +, then Add Action, and choose "Open URLs". Paste the link.
    3. Name the shortcut "SOS" and tap Done.
    4. For a home-screen button: tap the shortcut's share icon, then "Add to Home Screen".
    5. For a widget: long-press the home screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, pick Shortcuts and choose "SOS".
    iPhone: double-tap the back of your phone for SOS
    1. Make the "SOS" shortcut above first.
    2. Open Settings, then Accessibility, then Touch, then Back Tap.
    3. Pick Double Tap or Triple Tap and choose "SOS".
    4. On iPhone 15 Pro and newer you can also set the Action Button to the "SOS" shortcut in Settings, then Action Button.
    Android: SOS, Walk with me and Fake call shortcuts
    1. Install Shecurity from Chrome first (three dots, then Install app).
    2. Long-press the Shecurity icon. You'll see SOS, Walk with me, Check-in and Fake call.
    3. Drag any of them onto your home screen to get its own one-tap button.
    Add Shecurity to your home screen
    • iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". Needed for alerts on iPhone.
    • Android (Chrome): tap the three dots, then "Install app".

    SOS settings

    Free forever

    Every safety feature in Shecurity is free and always will be: SOS, your circle, live location, check-ins, fake call and siren. No ads, ever, because nothing should get in the way in an emergency.

    Shecurity is a profit-free project: nobody earns money from it. If you'd like to help pay for future upgrades, you can give any amount you like. Every bit goes into making Shecurity better.

    Support future upgrades

    Donating is completely optional and changes nothing in the app.

    Shecurity Plus

    Coming later

    Optional extras for people who want more, about $2.99 a month. They help keep the free app running.

    • Automatic texts to people who don't have Shecurity
    • 24/7 professional help that can send emergency services
    • Recordings and alert history kept for a year
    • A friendly AI voice that keeps you company on the walk home

    Privacy

    • Only people in your circles can see your name, alerts and location.
    • Your location is shared only during an SOS, a walk, a ride or a missed check-in, and stops automatically.
    • Alerts are deleted after 30 days. Backup contacts and settings stay on this phone.
    • No ads, no tracking, no selling data. Ever.
