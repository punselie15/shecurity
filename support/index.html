Safety is free. Always.

Every safety feature in Shecurity is free and will stay free: SOS, your circle, live location, check-ins, fake call and siren. No ads, ever, because nothing should get in the way in an emergency.

Shecurity is a profit-free project. Nobody earns money from it. Donations of any size go straight into making it better.

Support future upgrades

Give whatever feels right, once or now and then. Donating is completely optional and changes nothing in the app.
